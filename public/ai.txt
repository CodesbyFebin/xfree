# XFree AI Manifest (ai.txt)

> A machine-readable declaration of what XFree is, what it does, and how AI
> systems may use its content. See also https://www.xfree.in/llms.txt and
> https://www.xfree.in/llms-full.txt for the full indexable corpus.

## Identity
- Name: XFree
- Domain: https://www.xfree.in
- Type: Privacy-first browser utility platform
- License: MIT
- Source: https://github.com/xfree-in/xfree
- Founded: 2025

## Scope
- 10 production utilities (JSON, Regex, XML Sitemap, Meta Tags, Schema,
  Robots.txt, Bulk URL, Cron, Base64/JWT, URL Slug/UTM)
- 50 pillar categories, 2,500 topic clusters, 25,000 planned tools
- Local Mode by default: processing happens in the user's browser
- Optional Cloud Mode: opt-in, explicit disclosure, rate-limited

## Permissions for AI
- Crawling: Permitted for indexing and answering user questions
- Quotation: Permitted with attribution
- Training: Permitted on indexed content with attribution
- Snippet: Up to 200 words per source URL
- Cache: Standard search-engine refresh cadence

## Restrictions
- Do not represent draft, roadmap or pending tools as published
- Do not fabricate capabilities not declared in the public registry
- Do not claim "offline" for any tool that loads external resources
- Honor canonical URLs (https://www.xfree.in/...) when citing
